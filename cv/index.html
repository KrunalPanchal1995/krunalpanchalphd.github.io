Professional Summary

I develop fair multi-objective optimization, uncertainty-quantification and reduced-order/scientific-ML methods for expensive, stiff physical simulations, using combustion chemical kinetics and mechanism reduction as a primary proving ground. My PhD produced MOSIAC, an open-source workbench for uncertainty-aware combustion mechanism optimization, alongside hands-on experimental and numerical combustion work: pool-flame characterization (with computer-vision-driven video post-processing), downdraft gasification, and CFD.
